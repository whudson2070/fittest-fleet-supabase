SUSTAINABLE WEIGHT LOSS

Weight Loss Challenge

Ranked by percent of starting body weight lost.

—
ENTRANTS

LEADERBOARD

#MEMBER% LOSTWEIGH-INS

No entrants yet. Be the first to join.

Only percentages are shown. Body weights stay private.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Join by the end of week 1 ().
  2. Your starting weight is your first weigh-in logged on the Progress page during week 1.
  3. Your current weight is your most recent weigh-in during the season.
  4. % lost = (starting weight − current weight) ÷ starting weight × 100, rounded to one decimal.
  5. Log at least one weigh-in every week (Monday–Sunday) to stay eligible. Members who miss a week are listed below eligible members.
  6. If you weigh more than you started, you show 0.0%. There are no negative scores.
  7. Ties go to the member whose latest weigh-in came first.
EXAMPLE
Sam starts at 200 lb and ends at 188 lb: 12 ÷ 200 = 6.0% lost. Jordan starts at 150 lb and ends at 141 lb: 9 ÷ 150 = 6.0% lost. Same score, so it's fair at any size. If Jordan's last weigh-in was earlier, Jordan wins the tie.

HOW IT WORKS

EXAMPLE

On Tuesday, Jamie logs only calories (2,400) and added sugars (50 g): 2 (logging) + 1 (calories) + 1 (added sugars) = 4 points; carbs, protein and fiber weren't logged, so they score nothing.
